# Privacy Policy — Fire Lord 十三张 Chinese Poker

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Fire Lord 十三张 Chinese Poker ("the game") is a single-player card game for Android. This policy explains what the game does with your information. In short: **nothing leaves your device.**

## Information we collect
**None.** The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or device information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. The app does not request internet access.

## Information stored on your device
Your progress (play chips, jade, relics, achievements, settings and records) is saved only on your device, in the game's private app storage. No one else, including the developer, can see it.

Android may include this app data in your own device backup (for example, Google's Android backup), under your Google account and your device's backup settings. That backup is controlled by you and Google, not by the game.

## Play money only
Chips and jade are play money. They cannot be bought with real money, have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for anything outside the game.

## Children
The game is intended for adults (18+) because it includes simulated casino gambling. It is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

## Deleting your data
Uninstall the game, or clear its storage in Android Settings › Apps › Fire Lord 十三张 › Storage. This permanently deletes all game data.

## Changes to this policy
If the game ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated first, with a new date above.
